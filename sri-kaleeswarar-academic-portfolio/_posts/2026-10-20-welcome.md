This portfolio is a public notebook for my ongoing work across physics, scientific computing, open-source software, and science communication.

I plan to use it to document projects clearly: what question motivated the work, what methods I used, what the results do and do not show, and where the code or supporting materials can be found.

The site will grow as I add project notes, writing links, lectures, and verified milestones. Work in progress will be labelled as such, and I will aim to make the materials reproducible and accessible.
